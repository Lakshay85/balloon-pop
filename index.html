How to play

Pop balloons

Tap balloons before they float away. Normal balloon: +10 points.

Golden balloons

Fast and rare — worth +50 points. Grab them before they escape!

Dodge the bombs

Don't tap a bomb — each one costs a life. You have 3 lives per level.

Build a combo

Pop balloons back-to-back to raise your combo multiplier and chase the high score.

Features

  • 10 levels that get faster and harder
  • 1–3 star rating on every level
  • Level select — replay any level you have unlocked
  • 60-second timer on each level
  • Pause, resume and restart anytime
  • Sound effects you can turn on or off
  • Light and quick — a tiny download, works offline
  • Free, no ads, no sign-up, no account needed
