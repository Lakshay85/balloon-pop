Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Balloon Pop is a casual balloon-popping game. This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with your information.

Information we collect

We (Midnight Tales) do not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information. The app does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data. The one exception is the optional rewarded ad described below, which is served by Google and governed by Google's own policies.

Data stored on your device

The app stores only game progress on your own device (unlocked levels, star ratings and high scores), using your device's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

Advertising

Balloon Pop shows no banner ads and forces no ads on you. After a game over you may optionally choose to watch a short rewarded ad to revive your run (one heart back and extra time). Ads are served by Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

If you choose to watch an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share certain data with Google, including your device's advertising ID, IP address (used to estimate coarse location), device and app information, your interactions with the ad, and crash or diagnostic data. Google describes exactly what its SDK collects, per SDK version, at developers.google.com/admob/android/play-data-disclosure. Use of that data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, and you can control ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com. If you never watch an ad, no data is collected or shared by the ad SDK.

Analytics

Balloon Pop uses no analytics and no tracking tools.

Accounts and sign-in

The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no social features. There is no chat and no user-generated content.

Children

The app itself collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Optional rewarded ads are served by Google under its own policies.

Permissions

Balloon Pop does not request any sensitive device permissions. It connects to the internet only to load the optional rewarded ads described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here and the "Last updated" date will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app: contact the developer through the Google Play store listing.
